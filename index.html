How it works

A simple path from first message to a live site — and support after launch.

  1. Free intro call
  2. Fixed-price quote
  3. Build and delivery
  4. Support and maintenance
Step 115 minutes · Free of charge

Free intro call

A short call about the business: who your customers are, how they find you today, and what the site needs to do for you.

  • By phone, video or WhatsApp, whatever suits you
  • We look at your current site or social pages
  • Honest advice, with no commitment
Step 2One clear price

Fixed-price quote

You get one fixed price with everything written down: which pages, which features, and when it will be ready.

  • No hourly billing and no surprises
  • Everything included is listed in the quote
  • Work starts only after you approve
Step 3Usually 7–14 days

Build and delivery

We design and build the site, and you follow the progress and ask for changes before anything goes live.

  • Timing starts once you send the texts and photos
  • A preview link to check everything on your phone
  • Launch with the Google setup and a WhatsApp button
Step 4After launch

Support and maintenance

We stay with you after launch, so the site keeps working, stays up to date and grows with the business.

  • Small tweaks after launch are part of support
  • Optional monthly maintenance, from ₪150
  • The domain, the site and the content are yours
