Why your business is not showing up on Google

Most small business sites are invisible for the searches that matter: the service plus the area. Usually it is not bad luck. The site just never tells Google what the business does.

Google cannot guess what you do

Many sites open with a big photo, a “welcome” line and a phone number. That looks fine to a person, but Google needs plain words: which service, in which area, and for whom. If the page never says it, there is nothing to match to the search.

What local SEO actually means

A clear page for each main service. Page titles and descriptions written the way customers search. Business details that match everywhere they appear. A site that opens fast on a phone. None of it is magic. It is making the site easy for Google to understand.

A real example

Retail AVR started with almost no visits from Google. After the site and local SEO work, between October 2025 and July 2026 the site showed up in Google 58,700 times and got 2,870 visits. It usually appears on the first page, and about 1 in 20 people who see it click through.

How long it takes

Local SEO usually takes a few weeks to show. We track it in Search Console so you can see it too, instead of taking our word for it.
